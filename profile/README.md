# Chrome DAO

One Chrome NFT auctioned every day on Solana. The proceeds fund public-good initiatives backed by the community.

**Site** [chromedao.xyz](https://www.chromedao.xyz) · **Open ledger** [chromedao.xyz/dao](https://www.chromedao.xyz/dao) · **Discord** [CHROMES DAO](https://discord.gg/3yWKxcwp7Z)

## How it works

- **Daily auction.** One Chrome every 24 hours (rollover 00:01 Paris). Bids are paid in SOL; losing bids are refunded at settlement.
- **Split.** Every sale is split 50% DAO treasury, 40% builders, 10% system. Every split, refund and burn is public on [the DAO page](https://www.chromedao.xyz/dao).
- **Burns.** A Chrome nobody bids on and nobody earns on the action board is burned. Supply only goes down.
- **Initiatives.** The treasury funds [initiatives](https://www.chromedao.xyz/initiatives); anyone can back one with their wallet.
